FC Mee Sakul Serif

Classic luxury typeface, expressed with subtle sophistication.

Designed as a twin typeface system, typeface system pairs FC Mee Sakul, a Sans-Serif without serifs, with FC Mee Sakul Serif, its Serif counterpart. Both typefaces share the same underlying structure while expressing distinctly different moods.

The serifs add a sense of refinement, heritage, and credibility, evoking the timeless character of traditional typography. Rather than simply adding serifs to the existing letterforms, the design carefully rebalances both the internal and external spaces of each character. This allows the serifs to become an integral part of the form perfectly.

FC Mee Sakul Serif comes with 18 styles, consisting of 9 weight variations and their matching italics. A Variable Font version is also available.
